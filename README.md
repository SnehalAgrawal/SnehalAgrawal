## Case studies

Production systems I have built or led, written up at [snehalagrawal.com/work](https://snehalagrawal.com/work/):

- **[Real-Time AI Voice Agents](https://snehalagrawal.com/work/real-time-ai-voice-agents/)** (Fundamento): A voice agent platform that holds live phone conversations through speech-to-text, an LLM, and text-to-speech, in multiple Indian languages, across millions of calls.
- **[Agent Platform and Text-to-SQL](https://snehalagrawal.com/work/agent-platform-and-text-to-sql/)** (Trackonomy): A modular platform for building and running reusable, versioned AI agents, and a Text-to-SQL system that turns plain-English questions into analytics queries.
- **[AI Sales-Enablement Platform](https://snehalagrawal.com/work/ai-sales-enablement-platform/)** (Hupo): AI roleplay and coaching for enterprise sales teams across Asia, with review and approval workflows for regulated insurance and financial-services clients.
- **[Distributed Document Processing](https://snehalagrawal.com/work/distributed-document-processing/)** (47Billion): A document-processing platform for the mortgage industry, with runtime-configurable workflows and services that scale independently.
- **[AboutReact](https://snehalagrawal.com/work/aboutreact/)**: A React Native tutorial site I built and ran for five years, growing to 20K–25K daily visitors at peak, 92% of them from search.
